# Rooted Pantry Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 25, 2026**

Rooted Pantry does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The iOS app does not require an account, and pantry items, shopping lists, preferences, and reminder schedules stay on your device.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores pantry items, shopping-list entries, preferences, and reminder schedules locally so they remain available between sessions. Deleting the app removes this locally stored data.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules notifications locally through iOS. Rooted Pantry does not send notification data to a server.

## Network Access

The app works offline and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection services.
