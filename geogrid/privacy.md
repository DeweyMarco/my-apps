# Atlas Grid Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 24, 2026**

Atlas Grid does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The iOS app does not require an account, and all game progress, statistics, and preferences stay on your device.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The iOS app stores game progress, scores, streaks, statistics, and preferences locally so your games can continue between sessions. Deleting the app removes this locally stored data.

## Network Access

The iOS app ships with its game data and works offline. It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection services.
